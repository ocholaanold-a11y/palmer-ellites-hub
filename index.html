FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

What types of work does PEH provide?

Essay-writing guidance, research and dissertation coaching, business writing, Excel, PowerPoint and Canva designs, website analysis, visualization, SPSS, editing, tutoring, report checking, referencing and revisions. Assessed work remains the student's own responsibility.

Can I choose a particular specialist?

Yes, you can indicate a preferred writer in your quote request. The team will confirm availability and suitability.

Can I choose a payment currency?

Yes. Select your preferred currency when requesting a quote. Checkout is not active and no currency conversion is being made yet.

How do I pay?

Secure international checkout will be enabled after a payment provider is approved, configured and tested. This version does not collect card details.

Can high school students access PEH?

High-school learners are welcome to explore services, but under-18 account registration and payments require appropriate guardian-consent and child-privacy safeguards before activation.

How are qualifications and achievements verified?

Profiles are only published as verified once PEH checks the professional's credentials and receives permission to display their information and portfolio samples.
